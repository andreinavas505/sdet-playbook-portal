1. The $130k–$190k+ SDET Archetype: What U.S. Engineering Directors Look For

In top-tier U.S. engineering organizations, the line between Software Engineer (SWE) and Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) has blurred. Companies paying above $130,000 USD do not hire script-writers who merely convert manual test cases into automation steps. They hire Reliability & Velocity Architects who can:

1. Build Test Infrastructure

Design containerized execution grids, ephemeral test environments, and parallel execution pipelines that reduce CI runtimes to under 15 minutes.

2. Shift-Left Quality Gates

Implement consumer-driven contract testing, static code analysis, and branch-level verification so regressions never reach staging.

3. Speak Business & Dev Metrics

Articulate quality impact in terms of MTTR (Mean Time to Resolution), deployment frequency, escaped defect rate, and engineering hours saved.

2. Inside the ATS Parsing Engine (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday)

Applicant Tracking Systems do not read your resume with human intuition. They utilize deterministic text tokenizers and NLP entity extraction to build an internal relational record of your candidacy:

// What Greenhouse / Lever extracts when parsing your PDF:
entity_type: "TITLE" → Extracted: "Senior SDET" (Passes boolean query: SDET | "Software Development Engineer in Test")
entity_type: "PRIMARY_STACK" → Extracted: ["Playwright", "TypeScript", "Docker", "GitHub Actions"]
entity_type: "METRIC_DENSITY" → Found: "65% CI runtime reduction", "0% defect escape rate"
// Fatal Error: Multi-column layouts break parser reading order, corrupting keyword tokens into gibberish.
